Who dies on American roads

In 2024, motor vehicles killed 8,155 people who were not in a motor vehicle.

Pedestrians and cyclists were 1 in 5 of everyone killed on US roads — people who imposed no risk on anyone else and had no steel cage around them. This is about the harm a mode of transport does to other people, not about which mode is safest to be inside of.

Sources: totals and pedestrian deaths from NHTSA, Overview of Motor Vehicle Traffic Crashes in 2024 (DOT HS 813 791); cyclist and motorcyclist deaths from IIHS Fatality Facts 2024. “People inside motor vehicles” is the remainder of the 39,254 total after the categories listed, and includes a small number of other non-occupants.
